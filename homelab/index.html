Building a homelab from scratch

After a few years away from iOS research, I've moved on to networking and security. This post is the start of a running log for a home server built from scratch, from the hardware up. I'm documenting all of it here and on GitHub as I go, mostly so future me can figure out what past me did.

The hardware

  • Lenovo M920s (i5-8500), bumped from 8GB to 32GB of RAM pulled from my PC
  • 256GB SSD for now, 500GB/1TB upgrade planned
  • NETGEAR GS308E managed switch
  • NETGEAR R6400v2 router

The plan

Proxmox VE as the hypervisor, chosen over free ESXi, with OPNsense running as the firewall VM. The whole lab stays isolated from the rest of the household network.

Next up: installing Proxmox and getting OPNsense sitting between the lab and everything else. Stay tuned.
