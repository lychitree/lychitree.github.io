Building a homelab from scratch

In pursuit of real-world NOC and SOC experience, I've begun building a homelab from the ground up and documenting every step. The goal is to put concepts from my university courses and my CCNA and CompTIA Security+ studies into practice on real hardware, from firewall and switch configuration to network monitoring and threat detection. Each part of the build gets its own writeup below.
